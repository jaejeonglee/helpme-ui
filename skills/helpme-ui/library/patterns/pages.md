# Pages

Use when building or reviewing a landing, product, pricing or sign-up page that has to explain the product before anyone uses it.

## First screen answers four things

```
매장 예약을 문자 대신 앱 하나로        what (a plain noun line)
직원 10명 이하 매장을 위한 예약·알림   for whom
[무료로 시작하기]   요금 보기           next: one main action, one quiet one
[real 예약 화면, phone frame]           the product itself
```
- Show the real product: a screenshot, a short loop or a live demo. Not a stock photo, not an abstract shape.
- Slogans go under the what line, never instead of it.
- Cover the logo: if a rival could ship the same hero, rework it from the product's own nouns.
- Phone: headline, button and part of the product inside the first viewport; image below the text.

## Body

- One message per section; each earns its place or goes (`ONE-JOB`).
- Order (house default): what → how it works, in product screens → proof → price / FAQ → the main action again.
- Copy in the product's words, no 「혁신적인」, 「차세대」: [ux-copy](../korean/ux-copy.md).

## Proof must be sourced

- Logos: customers who agreed, in writing. No placeholder or "trusted by" rows.
- Numbers carry a sample and a period:
  `예약 누락 [n]% 감소 (고객 [n]곳, [기간], 자체 집계)`.
- Quotes: real person, real role, with consent. Else none.
- No proof yet? Show the product working instead and log the gap in `.helpme/plan.md`. Never fill it in.
- Third-party images: an `asset:` line in `.helpme/ledger.md` (source, licence).

## Check

- Capture phone and desktop; look at the first viewport alone. Can someone say what it is, for whom, and what to press?
- States still apply: form errors, sent, offline ([states](states.md)).
